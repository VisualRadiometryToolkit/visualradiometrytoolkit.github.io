It extends the [Visual Geometry Toolkit](https://visualgeometrytoolkit.github.io)
(James Pritts, Hendrik Sauer and contributors), where its first radiometric
types were developed, and the RadiometricCamera package of
[Real Cameras are Radiometric](https://realcamerasareradiometric.github.io)
(Ties Harder, Felix Seegräber, David Nakath, Hendrik Sauer, Kevin Köser, James
Pritts).
