Ecosystem

Radiometry lifts geometry. The toolkit builds on the Visual Geometry Toolkit (the blue node, its foundation): a radiometric camera wraps one of its cameras and adds what decides how much light each pixel collects. The dependency runs one way; no package of the Visual Geometry Toolkit depends on this one. The amber node is a related package, maintained outside the toolkit; the dashed arrows are dependencies being built (its port onto the toolkit), and the dashed pill is the paper it accompanies. Click a node to open it.

Packages

VisualRadiometryToolkitCore.jl

The types of a radiometric camera: a camera of the Visual Geometry Toolkit together with the parts that decide how much light a pixel collects (its focus surface, its aperture and its vignetting), with the scene it sees: lights, materials and a textured target.

pkg> add VisualRadiometryToolkitCore · registration in General pending

docscode

VisualRadiometryToolkitIO.jl

File formats for the toolkit: vignetting fields, environment lights and radial profiles as self-describing JSON files, in the envelope of VisualGeometryToolkitIO, which declares their units and conventions.

pkg> add VisualRadiometryToolkitIO · registration in General pending

docscode

Foundation

What the toolkit builds on, and whose conventions (naming, signatures, frames, units, Jacobians) it follows.

Visual Geometry Toolkit

Julia packages for multi-view geometry: coordinate frames, camera and lens models, geometric primitives, features, robust estimation and file formats, with physical units throughout. Its core supplies the cameras a radiometric camera lifts, RAW reading and the sensor noise model.

pkg> add VisualGeometryToolkitCore · General

Related packages

Packages in the toolkit's field, maintained outside of it.

RadiometricCamera.jl

The package of Real Cameras are Radiometric: lifts any central camera model of the Visual Geometry Toolkit to a radiometric one, with a finite aperture, a free-form focus surface and a generic vignetting function, and calibrates it by inverse rendering on the RAW photosites of ordinary calibration images. Its camera model is moving into this toolkit.

pkg> add https://github.com/RealCamerasAreRadiometric/realcamerasareradiometric.github.io:RadiometricCamera · General pending

Get started

The packages are at their first release, 0.1.0; their registration in the General registry is pending. They require Julia 1.13.

Radiometric calibration today

pkg> add https://github.com/RealCamerasAreRadiometric/realcamerasareradiometric.github.io:RadiometricCamera

The toolkit's core, from this repository

pkg> add https://github.com/VisualRadiometryToolkit/visualradiometrytoolkit.github.io:VisualRadiometryToolkitCore

Cameras, lenses, RAW images and sensor noise

pkg> add VisualGeometryToolkitCore

There is no meta-package: add the package for what you want to do, and its dependencies come with it. Every dependency of the toolkit is in the Julia General registry.
